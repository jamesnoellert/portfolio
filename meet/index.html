Meet

A small, simple call for up to four people. No account, no app, nothing to install.

  • Voice first. Video is there if you want it, kept small.
  • Eye contact. Faces sit at the very top of your screen, right under your camera, so when you look at someone it looks like you're looking at them.
  • Private. Audio, video and chat go straight between your browsers, encrypted. Nothing is recorded or stored. Close the tab and it's gone.
  • A chat for links and notes. Save it as a text file before you leave if you want to keep it.

A small public server (PeerJS) only introduces the browsers to each other. It never sees or hears the call.
